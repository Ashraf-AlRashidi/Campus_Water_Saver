OUR SHARED COMMITMENT

Water is a resource.
Not something to waste.

Awareness is where change begins. Your daily choices matter just as much as the systems around you.

Learn how to save water ↗
LEARN & PRACTICE

Make every drop
go further.

Simple habits can help reduce unnecessary water use. Start with one action and make it part of your routine.

01 / DAILY HABIT
⌁

Turn it off

Close taps while soaping your hands or brushing your teeth. Open them only when you need water.

↗
02 / BE OBSERVANT
◉

Notice the signs

Look out for dripping taps, running toilets, wet floors, or water where it should not be.

↗
03 / TAKE ACTION
＋

Report what you see

Do not ignore a leak. Share its location and a clear description through the report form.

↗
04 / THINK AHEAD
◒

Use water wisely

Carry a reusable bottle and avoid taking more water than you need.
